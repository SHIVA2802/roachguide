How to Prevent Cockroaches From Returning

Long-term control is easier when a home offers fewer food, water and hiding opportunities.

1. Control food sources

Store food securely, clean crumbs and spills, and avoid leaving pet food or food waste accessible for long periods.

2. Control moisture

Repair leaks and investigate damp areas. Kitchens, bathrooms and plumbing penetrations deserve particular attention.

3. Reduce hiding places

Keep storage organized and reduce unnecessary clutter in areas where pests can hide.

4. Seal practical entry points

Inspect gaps around doors, windows, pipes and other penetrations and seal suitable openings.

5. Monitor after treatment

Don't assume that seeing fewer insects immediately means the infestation has disappeared. Continue monitoring.

How to Deal With Cockroaches in a Kitchen

Kitchens combine food, water, warmth and hiding spaces, so control requires attention to all four.

Check the common hotspots

Inspect cabinet voids, areas beneath sinks, around appliances, plumbing penetrations and other dark, warm locations.

Remove food and water opportunities

Clean spills and crumbs, store food in closed containers, manage waste and repair leaks.

Use targeted treatment responsibly

Choose products appropriate for the situation and follow their labels. Keep pesticide products away from food and food-contact surfaces unless the label specifically permits the use.

Monitor

Use appropriate monitoring methods to determine whether activity is declining.

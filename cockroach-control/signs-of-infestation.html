Signs of a Cockroach Infestation

Seeing one cockroach does not automatically tell you how large the problem is. Look for patterns and supporting signs.

Common signs

  • Repeated sightings, especially at night
  • Small nymphs
  • Droppings or dark spotting in sheltered areas
  • Egg cases in suitable hiding locations
  • Unusual odour associated with substantial activity

Why nymphs matter

Finding young cockroaches can indicate that reproduction is occurring nearby, although a single observation is not enough to determine the full extent of an infestation.

What to do next

Map where activity occurs, reduce food and water access, select an appropriate control approach and monitor. See our main guide.
